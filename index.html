🍫 Chocolate Sponge Cake

⏱️ Prep Time: 15 mins
🔥 Cook Time: 30 mins
🍰 Servings: 8 people

🛒 Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 cup granulated sugar
  • ½ cup vegetable oil
  • 3 eggs (room temperature)
  • 1 cup whole milk
  • ⅓ cup unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 1 tablespoon baking powder
  • A pinch of salt and vanilla extract

👩‍🍳 Instructions

  1. Preheat your oven to 180°C (350°F).
  2. In a large bowl, beat the eggs and sugar with an electric mixer until creamy.
  3. Add the oil and milk and mix well.
  4. In another bowl, sift the flour, cocoa powder, baking powder, and salt together.
  5. Gradually add the dry ingredients to the wet mixture, mixing gently.
  6. Pour the batter into a greased baking pan and bake for 30 minutes.
  7. Let it cool completely before slicing. Serve with tea or coffee!
💡 Chef's Tip: Add a pinch of instant coffee to the cocoa powder for a richer chocolate flavor!
